# Data Analysis: Skills and Salary

An Excel analysis of data-related job postings, focusing on how in-demand skills vary across roles and how salaries compare by job, location, and skill.

## Workbook

**File:** `Project 2 - Data Analysis.xlsx`

The workbook contains summary analyses for roles such as Data Analyst, Data Scientist, Data Engineer, and related positions. It compares skill demand and median salary measures, including US and non-US salary summaries.

## Sheets

- **Skill_Job_Analysis** — compares skill likelihood across job titles
- **Salary vs Skills** — explores salary comparisons for selected skills
- **Salary analysis** — summarizes median salaries by job role and geography
- **Skil salary analysis** — compares salary measures across skills

## How to use

1. Open the workbook in Microsoft Excel.
2. Select a worksheet to explore skill demand or salary comparisons.
3. Use the available filters or slicers to focus on particular roles, skills, or groups, then compare the summaries.

## Notes

The salary and skill patterns reflect the job-posting data included in this workbook. Salary comparisons depend on the records and salary information available in the dataset and may not represent the full labor market.
