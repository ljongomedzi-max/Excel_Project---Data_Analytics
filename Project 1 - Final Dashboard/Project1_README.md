An interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard for exploring data job postings, salaries, and hiring trends. The workbook uses job-posting data to compare roles, locations, work arrangements, employers, and the skills listed in advertisements.

## Workbook

**File:** `Project 1 - Final Dashboard.xlsx`

The source data sheet contains 32,672 job-posting records with fields including:

- Job title and job title category
- Job and search locations, including country
- Posting platform and date
- Schedule type and work-from-home status
- Degree and health-insurance indicators
- Salary rate, annual salary, and hourly salary
- Company and listed job skills

## Sheets

- **Data** — source job-posting records
- **Data Validation** — supporting lists used for data validation
- **Title**, **Country**, **Type**, and **Platform** — supporting analysis by role, geography, job type, and posting platform
- **Salary Dashboard** — dashboard for exploring job and salary summaries

## How to use

1. Open the workbook in Microsoft Excel.
2. Go to **Salary Dashboard** and use the available filters or slicers to focus on a job category, location, or other dimension.
3. Compare the resulting summaries and refer to the **Data** sheet for the underlying postings.

## Notes

Salary and job-market patterns shown in the workbook reflect the included dataset. They may not represent the full labor market, and salary comparisons depend on the postings and salary information available in the data.
